## Company

**Ashford & Kane LLP** is a legal services company that handles a large number of client case requests through an online case intake system. Before assigning a case to a lawyer, the firm must evaluate whether the case should be accepted, rejected, assigned to a specialist, or require additional information. This evaluation helps reduce manual workload, improve response time, and ensure that each case is handled by the most appropriate legal expert.


## Problem

Ashford & Kane LLP receives hundreds of new case requests every week. Each case must go through an intake evaluation process before it can be assigned to a lawyer.

The decision cannot be made using a single condition because multiple factors interact with one another. These factors include conflict of interest, document completeness, evidence strength, case urgency, specialist availability, and the client's previous history with the firm.

The outcome of one evaluation step can change how later information is interpreted. For example, an urgent case with only missing secondary documents may be provisionally accepted, while the same missing documents in a non-urgent case may require requesting additional information. Likewise, a conflict suspicion may be acceptable when supported by strong evidence but require escalation when the available evidence is weak.

The goal is to determine the most appropriate action for each incoming case while considering the combined effect of all available information.
